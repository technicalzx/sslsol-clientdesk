  5. Turn research into action.

    At 26:30–27:37, research identifies companies and people; humans still lead relationships and execution.

Your daily 30-minute routine

01

Choose 3 relevant prospects

Use official websites. Look for audience overlap, a real problem and a public contact route.

02

Prepare one useful introduction

Research a page. Add your own observations. Draft a short message and check every claim before sending manually.

03

Learn from conversations

Record needs, objections and next steps. Improve the offer and your knowledge base after each call.

04

Follow up and measure

Track conversations, meetings, agreed pilots and clients won. Use observed results to decide which segment deserves more time.

Start with a pilot hypothesis

For SSLSOL, validate one workflow: backup readiness, ERP reporting, an AI assistant, or repetitive office tasks. Ask how the prospect handles it today. Agree a small pilot and measurable outcome after discovery.
